# Io and Jupiter textures

## Io

Credit: NASA/JPL/USGS. Galileo SSI / Voyager Color Merged Global Mosaic, 1 km.

- Source collection and metadata:
  https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/io_galileo_ssi_voyager_color_merged_global_mosaic_1km
- Full-resolution source (11445 × 5723):
  https://planetarymaps.usgs.gov/mosaic/Io_GalileoSSI-Voyager_Global_Mosaic_ClrMerge_1km.tif
- Related NASA description:
  https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/io-in-motion/
- Web asset: `io-8k.webp`, resampled to 8192 × 4096 and encoded as WebP
  at quality 94. The runtime fits the map to the device's texture budget.

The mosaic combines spacecraft images taken under different illumination
conditions. Its colors are enhanced relative to naked-eye appearance; the
renderer slightly reduces saturation and applies its own illustrative lighting.
The map's brightness is not used to invent terrain heights or volcanic hotspots.

## Jupiter

Map by Solar System Scope / INOVE, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

- Source collection: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Original image (4096 × 2048):
  https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/8k_jupiter.jpg
- Web asset: `jupiter-4k.webp`, encoded as WebP at quality 94. The filename uses
  the measured dimensions rather than the source URL's resolution label.

Lighting and orbital motion are illustrative. Jupiter's relative size and
separation are approximate; its viewing azimuth is composed for the viewport.
Attribution and license links are displayed on the alternate page.
