# Planet textures

Maps by Solar System Scope / INOVE, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

- Source collection: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- `saturn-4k.jpg` (4096 × 2048):
  https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/8k_saturn.jpg
- `saturn-rings-8k.png` (8192 × 500):
  https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/8k_saturn_ring_alpha.png

The collection is based on NASA imagery and adjusted to spacecraft photographs.
Downloaded images are unmodified. The surface file is named for its measured
dimensions, rather than the source URL's resolution label. At runtime, the
renderer extracts the ring map's central radial strip and may downsample maps
to the device's maximum texture size. Lighting and shading are applied in the
scene. Attribution and license links are also displayed on the alternate page.
